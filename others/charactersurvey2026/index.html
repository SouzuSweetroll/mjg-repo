Ninpo of the week

In January I set up a questionnaire for /mjg/ anons to fill out detailing their top 3 characters and all the characters they own, with some bonus questions at the end just for fun. I received 125 responses and here are the results. The planned release date of this was supposed to be at the beginning of February, but due to my ambition to trying to learn R/tidyverse, it has taken a bit longer than expected. Sorry about that. The questionnaires main aim was to find out the characters people use for fun, however I also added some fun questions to the end. The majority of the extra questions were optional and (to my surprise) most anons answered every question, so we have a surprising amount of data. However they weren't all optional and, as pointed out in the thread, some anons answering the questionnaire didn't want to answer everything; In hindsight I should've made all of the bonus questions optional. As a result it's best to take the data shown in this questionnaire with a pinch of salt. I will mention any outliers or cases where there were clear shitposts skewing the results (for example, an anon owning 600 character outfits) There were at least 2(?) character main questionnaires in the years before this in 2021 and 2023. If I find links to them I will add them here in this intro section too.
